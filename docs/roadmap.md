# Local SEO Apex Roadmap

## Vision

Local SEO Apex has three long-term goals:

1. Generate qualified leads for Local SEO Apex services.
2. Serve as the reusable Astro framework for future local business websites.
3. Become the marketing website for future AI software products.

# Long-Term Vision

Local SEO Apex is intended to become:

1. A lead generation website for SEO and digital marketing services.
2. A reusable SEO-first Astro framework used to build local business websites.
3. The public-facing website for a future suite of AI-powered software products serving home service businesses.
